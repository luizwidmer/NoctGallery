# Noct Gallery 0.6.0 App Store listing

Saved and submitted October 17, 2026, English (U.S.), build 14.

## Promotional text

Browse fullscreen, refine photos, edit video timelines and choose up to 4K/60 fps exports. Optional encrypted recovery keeps ordinary deletions for seven days.

## Description

Your photos. Your private space. Your choice of what to share.

Noct Gallery combines an encrypted photo and video gallery, a private camera and tools for preparing the copies you share.

FIND AND ORGANIZE
Open fullscreen with zoom, swipe navigation, a filmstrip and direct controls. Choose grid density and date grouping; use an iPad sidebar, albums, favorites, tags, captions and private notes. Search ranks matches, explains results and supports English date, media-type and album scopes. Review storage and duplicate suggestions yourself.

Optional text recognition makes text in photos searchable. Smart Search adds on-device English object and scene tags, editable synonyms and AI phrase interpretation. Smart features start off. Smart Search requires a supported device with Apple Intelligence enabled and ready. Generated tags can be wrong.

EDIT WITHOUT LOSING ORIGINALS
Crop with handles and aspect ratios, rotate, straighten and adjust brightness, contrast and saturation. Use undo, redo and original comparison. Encrypted edit recipes preserve original resources.

Import supported files from Files or use Save to NoctGallery from another app. Unlock to finish importing the encrypted incoming queue. Review individual outcomes, cancel pending work and retry unsuccessful files. The private camera offers focus, exposure, zoom, grid, flash and timer where supported.

REVIEW BEFORE SHARING
Blur details with adjustable local face, sensitive-text and QR/barcode suggestions. Move or resize areas, track video regions and edit blur keyframes. Use a video filmstrip, frame stepping, trim markers, waveform and audio muting. Suggestions and tracking can miss details: review the entire encoded copy before sharing.

Choose smaller video exports or higher quality up to 4K/60 fps, capped by source and device capability. Shared videos use H.264/AAC and SDR Rec.709; HDR is not preserved. Default video output is 1080p/30 fps. Inspect the actual export and its metadata before opening Apple's share sheet.

METADATA YOU CONTROL
Remove source metadata or apply replacement camera settings, dates and optional coordinates. Save sharing presets. Choose a place using Apple Maps without giving Gallery your current location. Captions and private notes are not added to exports.

KEEP SUPPORTED ORIGINALS
Move supported originals into encrypted storage without re-encoding, including supported Live Photo and RAW components. Gallery verifies every component before asking Photos to remove its copy. Unsupported or edited combinations remain in Photos. Copy to Private Gallery keeps the Photos source. The private camera saves inside Gallery.

PROTECTION YOU CONTROL
Use a PIN, supported biometrics, a compatible connected FIDO2 key or combinations. Key verification stays on-device. Optional Recently Deleted starts off and retains ordinary deletions for seven days when enabled. Restore items or remove them permanently sooner; expiry runs when Gallery opens or refreshes while unlocked. Duress and Purge and Reset bypass recovery and erase retained items.

LOCAL BY DESIGN
No app account, advertising, analytics or developer-operated media server. Private data is encrypted and excluded from backups. Credentials have no cloud recovery service.

Photos access is optional. Apple may download iCloud originals; Files providers may download selected files. Maps uses Apple's services. Share destinations receive copies they can retain or transmit. Editing and metadata removal do not guarantee anonymity.

Photos deletion can sync through iCloud Photos. Apple's Recently Deleted is separate from Gallery's optional recovery. Reset and duress do not erase Photos or previously shared copies.

English interface. All features work without a purchase. Optional one-time tips support development.

## App Review notes

Noct Gallery 0.6.0 (14) updates 0.5.0 (13). No account or sign-in is required. On first launch choose an app PIN, Save Protection, then Get Started. All features work without a hardware key. Use disposable media for deletion/duress checks. The interface is English.

BROWSE/SEARCH: Unlock > Private. Tap a photo to open fullscreen; use the filmstrip and favorite/edit/share/info controls. Library controls change grid density/date grouping; iPad has an album/tools sidebar. Search shows match reasons. Try "photos coffee last month", "type:video bicycle in 2025", or "album:Weekend" with appropriately dated/tagged media. Date/type/album scopes use actual stored metadata, independent of AI; only the remaining subject reaches optional local interpretation.

EDIT/VIDEO: From fullscreen open Edit Photo for crop handles, aspect ratios, brightness/contrast/saturation, undo/redo and original comparison. Prepare to Share opens blur/export controls. Detect Sensitive Areas and Detection Options offer local faces, text and QR/barcode suggestions. Video includes filmstrip, frame stepping, trim, blur keyframe navigation/removal, waveform and muted intervals. Timeline times refer to the original clip after trim. Video quality defaults to 1080p/30 fps; choose smaller or up to 4K/60 fps, limited by source/device. Output is H.264/AAC, SDR Rec.709 (no HDR preservation). Prepare Preview renders the actual encoded copy; review it completely before Share This Copy.

IMPORT/RECOVERY: Library Tools > Import from Files, or another app > Save to NoctGallery > unlock > Incoming Shares. Reports show individual results; retry handles unsuccessful items only. Cancellation stops pending work. The incoming queue is encrypted. Settings > Recently Deleted is OFF initially. Enable it before ordinary deletion to retain encrypted items for seven days, then Library Tools > Recently Deleted to restore or permanently delete. Expiry runs on unlock/foreground refresh while unlocked. Duress and Purge/Reset bypass retention and erase retained items immediately. This never deletes Photos or previously shared copies.

SMART FEATURES: Tagging, phrase interpretation and text indexing each start OFF. Library Tools > Smart Search is hidden on unsupported Apple Intelligence devices and gray/disabled while Apple Intelligence is off or the local model is not ready. When available, opt into Find objects and scenes, then optionally Interpret search phrases with AI. Import varied objects such as headphones, dog, bicycle, laptop, coffee and sunglasses. Tags are English. Edit Search Synonyms without retagging. Try "things you wear on your ears to hear music". Only the current phrase and a bounded tag vocabulary enter Apple's on-device language model; no cloud model/account/API key. Turning off tagging removes generated tags; locking cancels analysis and clears transient indexes. Private Text Search is a separate off-by-default OCR opt-in. Recognition/interpretation can be wrong.

PRIVACY: Encrypted originals remain unchanged by editing/sharing. Share copies are temporary. PIN, biometrics and compatible USB FIDO2 keys verify locally. No Smart Albums, backups, device transfer or Noctweave sharing. Optional tips unlock no features.

VALIDATION: 121 native iOS-on-Mac functional tests passed, including real Vision classification and local Apple AI inference, followed by focused viewer/recovery and encoded-video checks. Phone/tablet simulator regressions and native rendered review passed; unavailable simulator AI checks are explicitly skipped/excluded. Physical camera, Face ID and USB-key use were not verified. Screenshots show real app rendering with fictional generated photography.
